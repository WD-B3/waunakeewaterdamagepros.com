A frozen pipe is an emergency even before it leaks, because the split often hides behind ice and only releases water when the pipe thaws. If a faucet has stopped running in cold weather, open it and keep the heat on, and call the 24/7 line if you see wet spots, stains or dripping. Do not use an open flame to thaw a pipe.

Frozen pipe water damage is the flooding that follows when ice stretches a pipe past its limit and the pipe then thaws and leaks. The flood arrives on the warm-up, which is why a house can look fine at night and be soaked by afternoon.

Once the line has split, the water behaves like a burst pipe, with the same shutoff steps and the same drying. This page covers what is special about freezes.

Frozen pipe: what to do first

Act before the ice melts if you can.

  • Shut off the main water valve before the pipe thaws so a split cannot release water.
  • Open the faucet nearest the frozen section so melting ice has somewhere to drain.
  • Warm the area slowly with a space heater placed safely away from anything flammable, or a hair dryer. Never use a torch.
  • If you see water, shut off the main and call dispatch. Photograph the pipe, the wet areas and any stains.

How freezing damages a pipe

Water expands as it turns to ice, and the plug of ice pushes pressure down the line toward a closed faucet. The pipe usually splits along its length between the plug and the faucet, often in a spot that looks undamaged until the ice melts. Copper, PEX and PVC all fail this way, just at different points.

Where the pipe sits decides what gets wet. Exterior walls, attics, garages, crawl spaces and cabinets on outside walls are the usual locations. A split in an attic reaches the ceilings below, and one in a wall leaves water inside the cavity until it is opened.

How restoration works after a freeze

  • The crew confirms the water is off and the split is repaired, since repair belongs to a plumber.
  • Standing water is extracted, then moisture meters and thermal imaging find water trapped in walls and ceilings.
  • Wet insulation and drywall that cannot dry are removed, and the rest is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  • Readings are logged so there is a record for your insurer and a clear finish point for the job.

Large losses may continue into structural drying across several rooms.

Wisconsin cold and what it means

Madison's 1991 to 2020 normals put the January mean temperature at 19.4 degrees and the average January low at 11.8, and Waunakee shares that general climate. Cold snaps lasting several days are what turn a vulnerable pipe into a split one. Keeping cabinet doors open on outside walls and letting a faucet drip during a hard freeze are common ways to reduce the risk, and a plumber can advise on insulating exposed runs.

Frozen pipe questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Waunakee?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24 hours a day and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Cold snaps often hit overnight, so the line is staffed for it.

Should I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Slow warmth is safe, such as a hair dryer or a space heater placed carefully, with the main valve shut first. Never use an open flame, and call a plumber if the pipe is inside a wall.

Will the pipe burst when it thaws?

It can. The split often sits behind the ice plug and only leaks once the ice melts, so shutting off the main before the thaw is the safest step.

Does homeowners insurance cover frozen pipe damage?

Sudden and accidental freeze damage is often covered, and neglect may not be. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and keep photos and receipts.
