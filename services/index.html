Water Damage Restoration Services in Waunakee, WI

Nineteen services, grouped by where the water came from and what it reached. One 24/7 line covers all of them.

Water damage rarely arrives as a single, tidy problem. A burst pipe can soak a ceiling, a floor and a wall in the same hour, and the work that follows draws on several of the services below. Each page explains what that service involves, what to do while you wait and how the cost is built.

If you are not sure which page fits, call the 24/7 dispatch line and describe what you see. Dispatch routes the call to the right crew, and the order of work is explained before anything begins. The groups below follow the common starting points.

Pipes and plumbing

  • Burst Pipe Water DamagePressurized supply line failures, from first shut-off to repair.
  • Frozen Pipe Water DamageIce-blocked and thawed pipes, and the water they release.
  • Water Heater Leak CleanupTank failures and leaks, from shutoff to dry-out.
  • Slab Leak Water DamageLeaks in lines under a concrete slab, and the damage above.

Appliances and HVAC

  • Appliance Leak Water DamageWasher, dishwasher, fridge and ice maker line failures.
  • AC Leak Water DamageClogged condensate lines and HVAC leaks that wet ceilings and floors.
  • Kitchen Water Damage RestorationCabinets, flooring and subfloor after a sink or dishwasher leak.

Weather and building envelope

  • Ceiling Water Damage RepairStains, sagging and roof leaks through the finished ceiling.
  • Storm Water Damage CleanupWind-driven rain, hail and roof breaches, and the water inside.
  • Flood Damage CleanupRiver, groundwater and surface flooding, Category 2 and 3 water.
  • Basement Water Damage CleanupSump pump failures, seepage and wet finished basements.
  • Crawl Space Water DamageStanding water, wet insulation and joists in the under-floor void.

Water removal and drying

  • Water ExtractionPumps and extraction units remove standing water first.
  • Structural Drying and DehumidificationAir movers, dehumidifiers and moisture mapping to a dry standard.
  • Sewage Backup CleanupCategory 3 water, removal of porous materials and sanitizing.

Floors and walls

  • Hardwood Floor Water Damage RepairCupping, buckling and staining, with drying systems and repair.
  • Carpet Water Damage RestorationWet carpet and pad, extracted, dried or replaced by water category.
  • Wall and Drywall Water Damage RepairBubbling paint, soft drywall and wet cavities, opened, dried and repaired.

Commercial

  • Commercial Water Damage RestorationOffices, shops and multi-unit buildings, with minimal downtime.

Every service here follows the same order of work. The source is stopped, standing water is extracted, wet materials that cannot dry are removed and the structure is dried to a measured standard. Repairs come last, once the readings are dry.
