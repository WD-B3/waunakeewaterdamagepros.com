When water is spreading in Southbridge

Every minute of running water adds to the bill, so stopping it comes before cleanup. Use this short list while you call.

  • Shut off the water at the main, or at the supply valve for the fixture that failed.
  • Stay out of any room where water is near outlets, the panel or a running furnace.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics up and out of the water only where the floor is safe to walk on.
  • Do not use ordinary household fans or a shop vacuum on water that may be sewage or storm runoff.

Rain, drains and family homes

Residential plats are laid out with stormwater paths so that heavy rain can move away from homes. Even so, a hard storm can overwhelm a sump pump, a window well or a gutter, and water can enter at the lower level. We do not claim that Southbridge floods, only that every home near a drainage path should know how to respond.

If rain reaches the lower level, see basement water damage cleanup and storm water damage cleanup. If a drain backs up instead, sewage backup cleanup explains why that water needs a different approach and protective equipment.

Flood and storm context near Southbridge

Dane County's August 2018 flooding followed record rainfall, and county reports counted more than $154 million in damage and 1,544 residences affected. That is a county-wide figure, and it does not describe any one neighborhood.

Madison, the nearest official benchmark, averages about 37 inches of precipitation and 52 inches of snow a year, so spring melt and summer storms both test sump pumps and gutters. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.

Look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, then read your policy for sewer backup and sump overflow wording. Wisconsin insurance guidance says those events often need an added endorsement.

How a crew works a Southbridge job

  • The call starts with the address, what the water touched and whether anyone is in danger.
  • Crews pull out standing water first, because drying cannot begin until the bulk water is gone.
  • Moisture readings guide what comes out and what stays, and drying equipment runs until readings reach the dry standard.
  • Records of the work can help with a claim, while your insurer decides coverage.
