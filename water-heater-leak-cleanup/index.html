A leaking water heater is an emergency because a standard tank holds dozens of gallons that can drain onto the floor in minutes. Shut off the cold water supply valve on top of the tank, then turn off the power at the breaker or set a gas heater to pilot, and call the 24/7 line. Never reach into standing water near a powered heater.

Water heater leak cleanup is the removal and drying that follow a tank or fitting failure. The water is usually clean at first, which helps, but the location in a basement or utility closet means it reaches drains, framing and finished rooms nearby.

The source defines the page. A failed supply pipe belongs on the burst pipe page, and a washer or dishwasher belongs on the appliance leak page.

Water heater leaking: what to do first

Cut off supply and power, then keep clear.

  • Close the cold water inlet valve on the tank, usually on the pipe above it.
  • Turn off electricity at the breaker for an electric heater, or turn a gas heater to pilot. If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility.
  • Do not step into standing water near the heater unless power is confirmed off.
  • Photograph the tank, the label with its age, and the wet floor.

How a tank leak spreads

Water from a failing tank behaves differently from a pipe leak because the volume is large but slow at first. A weeping seam or corroded bottom can leak for weeks before it is noticed, soaking the floor and baseboards around the heater. A sudden failure drains the whole tank across the slab in a few minutes.

Tank heaters sit in basements, utility rooms and garages, so water often reaches the base of nearby walls and any finished rooms next to the mechanical space. That is why the first check after extraction is the wall bottoms, not just the floor.

Water heater leak sources

The source decides whether you repair or replace the unit.

SourceClueUsual fix
Tank seam or bottom failureWater pooling under the tankReplace the heater.
Pressure relief valveDripping from the discharge pipeReplace the valve and check pressure.
Inlet or outlet fittingDrips at the top connectionsRe-seal or replace the fitting.
Drain valveSlow leak at the low pointTighten or replace the valve.

How the cleanup works

  • The crew extracts standing water and checks the slab, wall bottoms and nearby rooms with moisture meters.
  • Baseboards and the lower drywall are removed if wet, and insulation behind them is checked.
  • Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the area until readings reach the dry standard.
  • Photos and readings are saved for your insurance claim, along with the heater label that shows its age.

The heater swap itself is a plumber's work. For a larger spread, see basement cleanup and structural drying.

What affects the cost of a water heater leak cleanup

Most of these losses are smaller than a pipe burst but vary with duration.

How long it leaked

A slow leak over weeks causes more damage than a fast drain caught at once.

Finished space nearby

Carpet, drywall and storage near the heater add scope.

Floor type

A bare slab dries quickly, while flooring over it takes longer.

Replacement

The heater itself is separate plumbing work.

Sudden tank failures are often covered by homeowners policies, while slow leaks and worn-out parts often are not. Ask your insurer.

Water heater leak questions

Do you handle water heater leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Waunakee?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24 hours a day and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Shut off the inlet valve and power first.

Can you replace the water heater?

Replacement is plumbing work. We clean up and dry after the leak and can work around your plumber.

How do I know how old my water heater is?

The label on the tank carries a serial number that encodes the manufacture date. Photograph it, since it may matter for your claim.

Is water heater leak damage covered by insurance?

Sudden failures are often covered and slow leaks often are not. Check your policy or ask your insurer.
