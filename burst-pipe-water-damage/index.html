A burst pipe is an emergency from the first second, because a pressurized supply line keeps pushing water until someone closes a valve. Go to your main water shutoff, usually where the line enters the house near the meter or the water heater, and turn it fully off. Then call the 24/7 line while water drains from the open faucets.

Burst pipe water damage is the wetting of walls, floors and ceilings by a failed supply line. It differs from a slow drip because the volume is large and fast, so water reaches framing, insulation and the floors below within minutes.

This page covers pressurized supply lines. Pipes that split because ice blocked them are handled on the frozen pipe page, and a failed tank is a water heater leak.

Burst pipe: what to do first

Seconds count more than neatness. Work through these in order while someone else calls.

  • Shut off the main water valve and open the lowest faucet to drain the lines.
  • If water is falling through light fixtures or near outlets, do not touch switches. Cut power at the panel only if you can reach it on dry floor, otherwise call your electric utility or 911.
  • Move valuables out of the path of the water and pull rugs off wet floors.
  • Photograph the break and the wet areas before anything is cleaned up.

Where burst pipes cause the most damage

The worst losses start where the pipe is hidden. A split inside an interior wall can run behind drywall for an hour before a stain shows, and a break in an upper-floor bathroom sends water through the floor into the ceiling below. A pipe under a sink releases water into the cabinet, then across the floor, which is why kitchen cabinets and flooring are common casualties.

Basement pipes put water on the slab, where it spreads in a thin sheet and runs into every low corner. That is a quick extraction job but an easy one to underestimate, because the water reaches the base of walls and finished rooms.

How the crew handles a burst pipe loss

The plumbing repair itself belongs to a plumber, so dispatch coordinates around it. The restoration work runs in this order.

  • Confirm the water is off, then extract standing water with pumps and extraction units.
  • Open the affected wall, ceiling or floor where moisture meters show water is trapped, since drywall and insulation hold water long after the surface looks dry.
  • Place air movers and dehumidifiers and log readings each visit until the framing meets the dry standard.
  • Document the break location, the wet areas and the readings for your insurance claim.

Causes of supply line failure

Knowing the cause helps your insurer and your plumber, and it helps you prevent a repeat.

Freeze splitCrack along the pipe wall, often in an exterior wall or atticSee the frozen pipe page, thaw slowly and repair.

What drives the cost of burst pipe cleanup

Volume and access matter most.

Run time before shutoff

The longer the pipe ran, the more rooms and floors the water reached.

Water category

A supply line is clean at first. Time and contact can change that, which changes the scope.

Materials wet

Insulation and drywall usually come out. Flooring may be dried in place.

Drying days

Wall cavities and floor assemblies decide how long equipment runs.

Repair scope

Rebuilding what was removed is estimated after the demolition is done.

Sudden and accidental pipe failures are the kind of loss homeowners policies commonly cover, but coverage depends on the cause and your policy. Ask your insurer, and call dispatch for a scope walkthrough.

A burst pipe in a Wisconsin winter

Madison's 1991 to 2020 normals put the January average temperature at 19.4 degrees, and Waunakee winters run in the same range. Pipes in exterior walls, garages and unheated crawl spaces take the hardest hits, and a break often shows up when a freeze ends and ice melts. If a pipe has already burst, shutting off the main valve now matters more than finding the exact split.

Burst pipe questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Waunakee?

Yes. The line is staffed 24 hours a day and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Shut off the main water valve and call, and dispatch takes you through the rest.

Do you repair the pipe itself?

The plumbing repair is a plumber's job. We handle extraction, drying and the building repairs that follow, and we work around the plumbing schedule.

Will homeowners insurance cover a burst pipe?

Sudden and accidental pipe failures are often covered, while gradual leaks usually are not. Check your policy or ask your insurer about the cause and the wording.

How long until walls are dry after a pipe burst?

Drying commonly takes several days, and moisture readings decide the finish. Wall cavities with insulation can take longer than open floors.
