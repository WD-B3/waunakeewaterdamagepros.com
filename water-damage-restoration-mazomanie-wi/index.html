What the 2017 hazard plan counts in Mazomanie

The Dane County hazard mitigation plan lists 19 Mazomanie structures in the 100-year floodplain, 17 of them residential, and 21 in the 500-year floodplain. It also notes that the village re-meandered Black Earth Creek to limit flooding and runs a cleanup program to keep debris out of the channel. Those projects help, but they do not remove the risk. A brick downtown and homes of many ages

The National Register nomination for the Mazomanie Downtown Historic District counts 36 resources built between 1859 and 1935, mostly two-story brick stores with stone foundations. The creek once powered a mill there. Older masonry buildings and stone foundations dry differently from modern framing, and moisture can sit in the walls for longer.

The homes around downtown span many eras. Older houses tend to have older plumbing, so burst pipes, frozen pipes and ceiling leaks are common calls, along with storm leaks through aging roofs.
