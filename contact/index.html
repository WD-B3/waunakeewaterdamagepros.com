Call now

The fastest way to reach a crew is to call. There is no form to fill in, because water damage does not wait for a reply. Describe what you see, give the address and dispatch tells you what to shut off while a crew is arranged.

For non-urgent questions, such as a report on past work or a question about this site, email is fine and is answered during business hours.

What to have ready when you call

  • The address of the loss and a callback number.
  • What happened and when it started, if you know.
  • Whether water is still running and whether you have found the shutoff.
  • Whether water is near electrical, the furnace or the panel.
  • Your insurance company name, if you plan to file a claim.

Calls to the dispatch line may be recorded for quality and training. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the call-taker at the start of the call.
