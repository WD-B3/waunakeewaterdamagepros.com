Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call, email or browse this site, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

This site has no forms and no accounts. When you call the dispatch line we collect what you tell us, such as your name, phone number, the address of the loss and a description of the damage. When you email us we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use it to keep the site running and to understand how it is used.

Call recording notice

Calls to the dispatch line may be recorded for quality and training. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the call-taker at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use call details to send a crew, to scope the job and to follow up on the work. We may share the details with the restoration crew and, at your request, with your insurer. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may show an embedded Google map. Those services may set their own cookies or log requests under their own policies. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by emailing the address on the contact page. We will respond within a reasonable time. This policy may be updated, and the date of the latest version is shown by the site build date.
