A ceiling that is bulging, dripping or stained brown is an emergency, because the water pooled above it has nowhere to go but down. Move people and furniture out from under a sagging section, shut off the water if a plumbing line is the source, and call the 24/7 line. Do not stand under a bulge, since saturated drywall can drop all at once.

Ceiling water damage repair covers the drying and rebuilding of a finished ceiling after water gets in from above. The source can be a roof leak, an upstairs bathroom, a supply line or an HVAC unit in the attic. This page also owns roof leaks that show up as ceiling damage, because the fix on the inside is the same.

If the water came from a pipe, see the pages on burst pipes and appliance leaks, since the source decides who repairs the leak.

Sagging ceiling: what to do first

Release the water safely and keep clear.

  • Keep everyone away from the bulge. Place a bucket under it and, if you can, poke a small hole with a screwdriver at the lowest point from the side to drain it.
  • Shut off the water if a plumbing line or appliance above is the source.
  • If water is reaching light fixtures or fans, do not touch them. Call your electric utility or 911 if the fixture is wet.
  • Photograph the stain, the source and any dripping, then call dispatch.

Roof leaks that appear as ceiling damage

A roof leak rarely stains the ceiling directly under the hole. Water enters at a flashing, valley or shingle gap, runs along the decking and rafters, and soaks insulation before it reaches the drywall. By the time a brown ring appears, the attic insulation and framing have often been wet for days.

Wisconsin winters add ice dams, which push meltwater under shingles at the eaves and into the wall tops and ceilings along the exterior walls. Repair of the roof itself belongs to a roofer. We handle the interior damage, and a quick tarp or roof repair stops more water coming in while we dry the structure. The same steps apply in Waunakee neighborhoods such as Westbridge.

How we repair a water-damaged ceiling

  • Moisture meters and thermal imaging confirm how far the water spread and whether insulation is wet.
  • Wet drywall that cannot dry is cut out, along with soaked insulation.
  • Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cavity and surrounding framing.
  • New drywall is hung, finished and primed once readings confirm dry framing, with painting as a separate step.

If the water traveled down a wall as well, wall and drywall repair covers that part of the job, and structural drying covers equipment and readings.

Ceiling water damage questions

Do you handle ceiling water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Waunakee?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day and same-day response is offered when crews are available. A bulging ceiling is a safety problem, so keep people clear while you call.

Does a roof leak have its own page?

No. A roof leak that damages the ceiling is handled here, because the interior repair is the same. A roofer repairs the roof itself.

Can a water-stained ceiling be painted over?

Not safely until the source is fixed and the area is dry. Stains bleed through paint, and wet drywall can grow mold, so drying and repair come first.

Will insurance cover a ceiling leak?

Sudden leaks are often covered, while slow leaks usually are not. Check your policy or ask your insurer.
