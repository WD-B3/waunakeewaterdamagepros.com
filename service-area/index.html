Waunakee and three nearby Dane County towns, each with its own page and the same 24/7 line.

The dispatch line is based at 200 W Main St in Waunakee, ZIP 53597, and crews cover the village and the nearby Dane County towns listed below. Dane, DeForest and Mazomanie each have their own page with local detail on flooding, housing and what to do first.

How coverage works

Dispatch is a single line for the whole area. You call, describe the water and give the address, and the call-taker tells you what to shut off while a crew is arranged. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and the first call is where that is confirmed.

Addresses outside the list are handled case by case. Distance, the type of loss and crew availability all matter, so the quickest answer is a short call.
