When water is spreading in Kilkenny Farms

Standing water or a spreading leak needs action before anything else. While you arrange help, work through these steps.

  • Close the main water valve, or the valve at the leaking fixture or appliance.
  • If water has reached outlets, the panel or the furnace, stay out and call your electric utility or 911 before you call us.
  • Photograph the damage from a dry spot, then lift small valuables and papers off wet floors.
  • Keep people and pets out of water that may be sewage, storm runoff or sump overflow.

How a crew works a Kilkenny Farms job

  • Work begins with a call about where the water came from, what it has reached and whether the area is safe to enter.
