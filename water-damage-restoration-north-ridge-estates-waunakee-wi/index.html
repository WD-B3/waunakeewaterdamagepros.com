When water is spreading in North Ridge Estates

Water that keeps spreading soaks drywall, subfloor and contents quickly. These steps limit the loss until a crew can start.

  • Look for the source first. A running tap, a split hose or a failed fitting can often be stopped at its own valve.
  • Never touch cords, breakers or appliances while standing in water. Wait for the utility if the panel is in a wet room.
  • Set aside papers, photos and electronics on a dry shelf, and record the damage on your phone.
  • Keep children and pets away from basement water and any water that smells like a drain.

Storms, roofs and lower levels

Wind-driven rain, hail and ice dams are the usual way storm water reaches a home from above. It enters at shingles, flashing or a vent, then runs into the attic and down through a ceiling. Water can also enter from below through a window well or a foundation crack after a long rain.

Start with storm water damage cleanup for roof and wall leaks, and ceiling water damage repair for what happens after water gets inside. For a lower level that took on rain, see basement water damage cleanup.

How a crew works a North Ridge Estates job

  • A dispatcher asks where the water started and what it touched, then explains what to do until the crew can start.
  • The crew removes standing water with pumps and extractors so that drying can begin.
  • Wet drywall, padding and insulation that cannot be dried are cut out, and the remaining structure is read with moisture meters.
  • Air movers and dehumidifiers run until the readings are dry, and you receive photos and a scope of work for your own records.
