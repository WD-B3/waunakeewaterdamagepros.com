Downtown Waunakee is the village center, the older part of town around Main Street where homes, apartments, offices and shops sit close together. Water that starts in one unit can reach another, so speed and clear communication matter more here than in a street of detached houses.

This page covers water damage in and around the village center. For the rest of town, see the Waunakee water damage restoration homepage and the full list of services.

When water is spreading in Downtown Waunakee

Standing water or a spreading leak needs action before anything else. While you arrange help, work through these steps.

  • Close the main water valve, or the valve at the leaking fixture or appliance.
  • If water has reached outlets, the panel or the furnace, stay out and call your electric utility or 911 before you call us.
  • Photograph the damage from a dry spot, then lift small valuables and papers off wet floors.
  • Keep people and pets out of water that may be sewage, storm runoff or sump overflow.

Where it is

The village center runs along Main Street. Village Park sits at 410 E Main St, beside Six Mile Creek, according to the Wisconsin Historical Society record for its bridges.

The creek

Six Mile Creek is the only stream named in the Dane County hazard plan for Waunakee. The plan lists the Community Center, park buildings and a village well as adjacent to it.

Downtown blends houses, apartments and commercial space. Building ages and basement use vary by lot and are not verified here.

Water problems in a mixed village center

In a dense core, the most common calls start inside buildings. A supply line fails under a sink, a water heater leaks into a utility room or a second-floor fixture drips into the unit below. These are plumbing failures, not floods, and they happen in any neighborhood.

For a wet basement or lower level, see basement water damage cleanup. For a storefront or an apartment building, commercial water damage restoration explains how containment and a written scope keep neighbors informed. For a pipe that splits in a cold snap, read about burst pipe water damage.

Flood and storm context near Downtown Waunakee

Dane County's August 2018 flooding followed record rainfall, and county reports counted more than $154 million in damage and 1,544 residences affected. That is a county-wide figure, and it does not describe any one neighborhood.

The county hazard plan counts one residential structure in the village in the 100-year floodplain and 22 structures, 20 of them residential, in the 500-year floodplain, with no structures in the floodway. Those are village-wide totals and do not say which blocks are affected. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.

Look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, then read your policy for sewer backup and sump overflow wording. Wisconsin insurance guidance says those events often need an added endorsement.

How a crew works a Downtown Waunakee job

  • Work begins with a call about where the water came from, what it has reached and whether the area is safe to enter.
  • Standing water is pumped or vacuumed out before any drying equipment is set.
  • Meters and thermal images show where moisture is hiding, and that map decides which materials are removed.
  • The crew leaves a written scope and readings that you can share with your insurer, who makes the coverage decision.

Water damage questions in Downtown Waunakee

Do you handle water damage in Downtown Waunakee apartments and shops?

Yes. Call with the address and describe what the water reached. Multi-unit and commercial jobs are planned around containment and a written scope. See commercial water damage restoration.

Is Downtown Waunakee in a flood zone?

Some parts of the village are mapped, but neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified here. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and remember that plumbing and roof leaks happen outside floodplains too.

What should I do first if a pipe bursts in my building?

Shut off the main valve, stay away from wet electrical equipment and photograph the damage. Then call so a crew can extract the water. Burst pipe water damage has the full steps.

How is water damage dried in an older building?

A crew maps moisture, removes materials that cannot dry and runs air movers and dehumidifiers until readings reach the dry standard. Structural drying explains the process.

Can you help document damage for an insurance claim?

Photos, moisture readings and a written scope can support your record. Whether the loss is covered depends on the cause and your policy, and your insurer decides.
