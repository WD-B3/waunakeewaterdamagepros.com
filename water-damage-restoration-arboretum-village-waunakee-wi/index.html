When water is spreading in Arboretum Village

A leak that is still running gets worse by the minute. Take these steps first, then call so a crew can plan the work.

  • Stop the source if you can reach the valve safely, and leave it alone if the floor around it is flooded.
  • Do not stand in water and touch breakers, cords or appliances. Call the utility if you are unsure.
  • Take photos of the damage and the source before anything is moved.
  • Treat basement water as contaminated until a professional says otherwise.
